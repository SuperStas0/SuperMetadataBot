*Hello*
This bot loads audio and lets you edit it before it is sent to you.
_Bot only works in DMs!_

*Loading audio*
Just type a text and the bot searches YouTube for it, or send a link, or send audio files.
Send a playlist, several links in one message or several files at once and they load together.

*Editing*
Change the title, performer and poster (thumbnail), or trim the audio by cutting out parts of it.

*Batch editing*
Audios loaded together can be edited at once: set one performer and poster for all of them, set the titles one after another, change the order and send them all. Every audio can also be edited on its own.

For full usage instructions use /help
